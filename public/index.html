• Unit-1 (Ionic Equilibria, Adsorption): pH scale, common-ion effect, Henderson-Hasselbalch equation, solubility product. Freundlich and Langmuir adsorption isotherm, BET theory.
• Unit-2 & 3 (Colloids & Equilibria): Lyophilic/lyophobic sols, CMC micelles, van't Hoff equation, Gibbs-Duhem equation.
